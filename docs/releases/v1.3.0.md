## What's new since v1.2.1

Gamepad support, feats and titles, a daily rift and fifty improvements in all.

### Gamepad
- Play with a controller: left stick moves, right stick aims, buttons cover every action slot, dodge,
  potion and interact. The last device you touched wins, so mouse and keyboard keep working.
- Menus, panels and the inventory work with the D-pad (A confirms, B backs, Start pauses, Select opens
  the map). Controller buttons can be rebound in Settings > Controls.
- Button prompts switch to controller glyphs, the controller rumbles on big hits and level-ups, and a
  toast shows when it connects or disconnects (the game pauses if it drops mid-fight).

### New things to do
- 21 feats with a list in the character panel; nine of them grant titles shown on character select.
- A daily rift with the same layout for everyone that day, plus best times per class and rift level.
- An escort bounty in every region.
- World bosses are announced and shown on the map with a countdown.
- The trader's stock can be rerolled for gold, and materials can be deposited in the shared stash.
- The Codex shows which dungeon drops each missing aspect.

### Feel and interface
- Level-up knocks nearby enemies back.
- The death screen lists the last hits that killed you.
- The guide line follows the real path and names where it leads; clicking somewhere unreachable shows
  a red X.
- Item tooltips open with a one-line summary of the comparison; ring arrows compare against your
  weaker ring.
- New settings: Reduce motion, Show tips and Reset tips.
- Sharper map at high zoom, a pickup sound for the loot magnet, greetings for quest NPCs.

### Balance and fixes
- Lightbearer: Halo of the Penitent keeps Sanctuary up about a third of the time; the Radiant aspect
  stacks less with Zealot; Holy Light, Heal and Magnus Exorcismus retuned slightly.
- Salvaging a junk-marked legendary now gives dust.
- The error screen has a "Copy details" button for bug reports.

Saves and accounts from v1.2.1 load unchanged.
