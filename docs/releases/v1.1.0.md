## What's new since v1.0.0

- **Endgame**: six endgame systems (see `docs/endgame.md`).
- **A fourth class, the Lightbearer**: a support priest who still deals damage. Heals and shields
  feed Devotion, Heal burns corrupted enemies, Guardian Lights follow and fight, Benediction keeps
  allies alive. 10 skills, a skill tree, new bases, aspects and uniques.
- **Mobile portrait UI**: 200 improvements for phones held upright.
- **Diablo-style UI pass** (100 items): desktop combat feedback, small life bars on damaged
  enemies, elite plates with affixes, minimap blips, element-coloured damage numbers, capped
  ground loot labels with a rarity filter, item lock and junk marks, side-by-side comparison
  tooltips, stash sort/filter/pages, vendor buyback, Esc pause menu, key rebinding, colour-blind
  palette, reduce flashing and brightness settings.
- **Equip best**: one button in the inventory equips every upgrade in your bag.

Saves from v1.0.0 are migrated automatically.
