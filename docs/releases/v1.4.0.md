## What's new since v1.3.1

Enemies, bosses and townsfolk that used to share a model now have their own look.

### New looks
- About 70 enemies and bosses across all five regions and the world bosses get their own parts and colours.
  - Fuel tanks on the Ash Thrower, a mitre and censer on the Smelter Priest, ice on the Frozen Welder.
  - Moss, vines and cocoons in the Vaults.
  - Helmet lamps on infected miners, drill arms on the crushers, crystal armour on the Prism.
  - Ghostly fragments on the Core's shades.
- The Lumen turret and the Heart of the Core have models of their own.
- Every named townsperson has their own look (hats, helmets, packs, coats), and the same person looks the same in
  every hub they visit.
- Loot on the ground is an item cache, a Lumen crystal or a pile of credits instead of a plain box; keycards and
  the rift obelisk have their own models.

### AYN Thor
- `ashfall-v1.4.0-thor.apk` installs over v1.3.1 and keeps your saves.

Saves and accounts from v1.3.1 load unchanged.
