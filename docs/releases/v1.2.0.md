## What's new since v1.1.0

A 100-item improvement pass across balance, content, performance and game feel.

### Balance
- **Lightbearer** tuned: Magnus Exorcismus no longer stacks into triple damage, Benediction's
  "cannot die" can't be kept up permanently, Holy Light hits harder, Guardian Lights matter, and the
  healer and shepherd builds level at the same pace as other classes.
- **Spectre** Blades survive energy damage, **Bastion** Furnace Cleave and Heat Vent are stronger,
  **Xenomant** disease does more single-target damage.
- Potions recharge faster as you kill, kill XP falls off when you outlevel a zone, more ranged
  enemies in regions 2-4, elites and strongholds scale with level, Deep Rift damage scales gentler
  and its rewards keep growing to rift 100.
- Dying now costs a little: 5 % of carried gold (capped) and half your potion charges.

### Content
- 10 new aspects, 4 uniques, 4 mythics (one for every class plus a classless one), 5 new armour and
  jewellery bases, a ranged damage stat and two new crystal types. Every class can use the Codex.
- 15 repeatable bounties after each region's story, two choice quests, item rewards on 24 side
  quests, varied Core and mystery quests, and NPCs whose greetings change as the story moves on.
- Force Wall and Laser Grid elite affixes, two new world bosses, four tempering recipes and two new
  Cinder Flats enemies.

### Performance and robustness
- Much faster start: models load per zone (about 3.6 MB before character select instead of 39 MB),
  zone changes no longer stall on the ground texture, game files are 74 → 55 MB, and the
  simulation is up to 20x cheaper per tick.
- Saves are safer: a damaged account no longer wipes the shared stash, failed autosaves are shown,
  corrupt or newer saves are never overwritten, and losing the graphics context is handled.

### Feel and audio
- Coach tips, a golden guide to the first objective and a move marker for new players; stronger hit
  feedback; a zone-change card with tips; a framed respawn; a level-up effect; a boss intro beat.
- The Lightbearer gets its own sounds, enemy attacks have distinct warning sounds, the Core has its
  own music, a combat drum layer joins when you're in danger, and loot sounds rise with rarity.

Saves from v1.1.0 are migrated automatically.
