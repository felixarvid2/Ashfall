## What's new since v1.2.0

A polish pass on the 100-item improvement pass, plus security updates.

### Faster and smoother
- Zone changes no longer wait for NPC and set-piece models after the fade: they load during it.
- Respawn and the zone-change card run on real time (respawn takes about 1.7 s on any device).
- Static props are placed once instead of every frame; three heavy panels load on first use.

### Gameplay and balance
- Lightbearer: late-game Devotion no longer stays near full; Vigil, Consecrated Mail and Sacred Oath
  now scale with your level (percentage life/armour, more damage against the corrupted).
- Bastion: Furnace Cleave reaches 3.9 m and Heat Vent hits harder per Heat.
- Gold and crystals near you fly to you.

### Quality of life
- New players see a recommended first skill in the skill tree.
- Enemies hidden behind tall props show a ring at their feet.
- The minimap marks chests, pylons and other interactables in their own colours.
- Your first completed quest shows exactly what it paid.
- Four new uniques: Lodestone Grips, Sapper's Signet, Gravecaller's Grasp and the Vigil Ring.

### Security
- Updated Vite and Zod to versions that fix published advisories.

Saves from v1.2.0 load unchanged.
