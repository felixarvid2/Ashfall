## What's new since v1.4.0

Four bosses get models of their own.

### New boss models
- **Commandant Hale** (Cinder Flats): riot armour, an officer's cap and a torn command coat, with Lumen crystal
  bursting through his shoulder and face.
- **Dr. Ilse Varga** (Hydroponic Vaults): a stained lab coat overgrown with moss, vines and flowers, spore canisters
  on her back.
- **The Archivist** (Deep Mines): a hooded figure of glowing crystal light in a robe of old glyphs.
- **Governor Kade** (Deep Mines, and his shade in the Core): gold-trimmed powered armour, a crown-like collar, a
  tattered governor's coat and a tower shield with the corporate seal.

### AYN Thor
