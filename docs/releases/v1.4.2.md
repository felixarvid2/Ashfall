## What's new since v1.4.1

Five more characters get models of their own.

### New models
- **Dr. Amara Okafor**: her own model in Lab 9, Station Zero and as the face Lumen takes, with a lab coat, round
  glasses, a respirator and seed pouches.
- **Infected miners** (Deep Mines): hard hats with headlamps, torn orange work suits, drills and dynamite, amber
  crystal growing through them.
- **Security troopers** (Deep Mines): blue-grey corporate armour with glowing visors.
- **Security Chief Holm** (Deep Mines boss): officer armour, a peaked cap and a shield generator on his back.
- **Brother Ash** (Refinery boss): blackened foundry armour cracked with molten light, a hood and a burning censer.

### AYN Thor
- `ashfall-v1.4.2-thor.apk` installs over v1.4.1 and keeps your saves.

Saves and accounts from v1.4.1 load unchanged.
