## What's new since v1.4.2

Six more enemies get bodies of their own, from a free CC0 model kit.

### New models
- **Rust, flame and governor drones**: a new armoured eye drone, rusted, ringed with a burner or plated in gold.
- **Slag hound** (Refinery District): a scuttling four-legged shell bot with slag plates.
- **The Drowned Engine** (Deep Mines boss) and **the Brood Engine** (the Core): a heavy disc-shaped walker with
  guns, hung with moss and cables or lit by the Lumen.

Models: Sci-Fi Essentials Kit by Quaternius (CC0).

### Downloads
- Ubuntu/Debian: `sudo apt install ./ashfall-v1.4.3-ubuntu-amd64.deb`, then start Ashfall from the app menu.
- AYN Thor: `ashfall-v1.4.3-thor.apk` installs over v1.4.2 and keeps your saves.

Saves and accounts from v1.4.2 load unchanged.
