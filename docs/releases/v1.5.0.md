## What's new since v1.4.3

Eighteen bosses get models of their own, the Core gets four new events, and about eighty improvements across
the game.

### New boss models
- Region bosses: the Pyre Warden, the Frozen Welder, Foreman Dray, the Brood Warden, the Keeper, the Elder
  Guardian, the Brood Mother, the Vine Matriarch, Gamma Bloom, the Warden (and its shade), Mother Root, the Prism
  and the Choir Mother.
- World bosses: the Ashen Colossus, the Mother of Spores, the Hollow Engine, the Choir of Stone and the Echo Engine.

### World and combat
- Four new events in the Core: a collapsing light bridge, a choir awakening, a memory surge and a gravity storm.
- New elite affixes: Force Wall and Laser Grid.
- Boss arenas seal with energy barriers during the fight.
- Four rift modifiers: Volatile Dead, Shrinking Arena, Elite Storm and Lumen Surges.
- Hardcore characters: one life, a skull on the slot and a separate stash.
- Death wears your gear (repair it at the blacksmith); lost Ash Shards wait where you fell for 5 minutes.
- A killed world boss stays dead after a reload; loot and running escorts or events wait a few minutes when you
  change zones; saves keep your life, resource and buffs.
- Core dungeons are shorter (about 11–12 min); Cinder Flats dungeons are a little longer and its roads less crowded.
- The camera keeps Lumen fully in view; two new weapon models in hand.

### Interface
- Loot filter, stash search and sorting, comparison tooltips in the stash and at the trader.
- Photo mode (F8), minimap zoom, pinned quests, a pickup log, damage number options and boss phase markers.
- UI scale, colour-blind rarity shapes, subtitles for NPC barks, accessible labels and "don't show again" on tips.
- Settings > Data: save backups, a checked save import and a copyable error log; an FPS cap and an automatic
  graphics preset on first run.

### Content
- Six new uniques, eight legendary aspects, three bounty types, four world events, twelve lore notes and ten
  feats (three with titles).
- Hub NPCs talk now and then; rare elites have names; bosses have signature drops.
- Weather and ambient life in every region, new props, decals, machine deaths, a bestiary and a credits page.
- New music in the Core: "Heavenly Loop" by isaiah658 (CC0).

### Performance and builds
- Distant enemies animate at a lower rate and only the nearest cast real shadows; a zone-change leak is fixed.
- The web build works offline; the zone loading card shows progress.
- Ubuntu: the app remembers its window and serves media in ranges. AYN Thor: Back asks before quitting.

### Downloads
- Ubuntu/Debian: `sudo apt install ./ashfall-v1.5.0-ubuntu-amd64.deb`.
- AYN Thor: `ashfall-v1.5.0-thor.apk` installs over v1.4.3 and keeps your saves.

Saves and accounts from v1.4.3 load unchanged.
